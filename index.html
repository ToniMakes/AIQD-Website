Desktop app privacy

Quota readings stay on your device.

  • The desktop app runs locally and serves its dashboard through 127.0.0.1.
  • Stores supported quota readings and refresh history on your device.
  • Does not upload prompts, responses, source code, or chat content from the desktop app.
  • Does not read browser cookies, sign in to accounts, or call private provider APIs.
  • The separate website feedback form sends the details and email address you choose to provide only when you submit it.
  • This website counts page views and link clicks by broad source category, button position and language. It stores no visitor IDs, IP addresses or full referrer URLs in its analytics database. Hosting services still process network information. Website statistics do not include desktop app activity.
  • You can disable website statistics using the footer control. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are respected. Disabling statistics does not remove previously aggregated counts.
